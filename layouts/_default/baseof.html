Richter Has Joined the Cardinal Family

After decades serving Watertown, Bill and Rosy Richter have partnered with Cardinal Heating, Cooling, Plumbing & Electric, a family-owned Wisconsin company trusted since 1984. Adam and Sarah Richter are staying on to lead the Watertown team, with the same office on Water Tower Court and the same phone number, 920-988-7050. Now you can count on Cardinal for plumbing and electrical service, too.
